Craig David Interview - Pineapple Tequila Cocktail

Craig David told me about a tequila and pineapple cocktail he couldn't stop thinking about.
We built him a serve with Teremana Reposado, fresh pineapple, and a hint of habanero.
This was filmed at Belmond Pullman during the Drinks With Fun People series.

Recipe:
50ml Teremana Reposado
25ml pineapple juice
10ml lime juice
2 dashes habanero tincture
Shake and fine strain. Garnish with charred pineapple wedge.
